# Mandership: reasoning for website decisions

[Mandership in English](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/) and [Ководство in Russian](https://www.artlebedev.ru/kovodstvo/sections/), by Artemy Lebedev. The original 23-essay selection was reviewed on 2026-11-01; Russian-source coverage and §§190–191 were added on 2026-11-08, bringing the selection to 25. This is not the entire collection. Each entry distinguishes the source position from this skill's application. Some essays are aphorisms, others contain developed arguments, and later entries sometimes revise earlier ones. Implementation obligations such as accessibility and authorization remain independent of aesthetic opinions.

## Use both source languages

Consult the Russian original when the English index lacks an entry, a translation is unavailable, or wording changes a design decision. Use the source's numbered essay and publication date; the indexes are not equivalent. On 2026-11-08 the English index ended at §189 (2018), while the Russian index included §190 (2018) and §191 (2019). These are historical publication dates, not a newly issued 2026 design standard. Check the indexes when freshness matters rather than treating this observation as permanent.

Retain the original title and URL when translating a Russian-only source; identify an English rendering as a translation. Attribute opinions to the author. Do not turn anecdotes, neuroscience language, rhetorical absolutes, or technical advice tied to old devices into contemporary empirical evidence. More recent publication does not automatically make an essay more useful for the current task.

Read by decision: task and rules; concept and references; information and navigation; editorial and typographic craft; simplicity and variety; mobile chronology.

## Task and rules

### §149 — Achieving tasks · 2008-07-08

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/149/): Frames design around accomplishing the task and recognizes that improving appearance can itself be the task. Describes mutual consideration and compromise with clients.

**Application:** Define what the work should achieve and review that result. Do not turn every appearance edit into a new business strategy, or insist every page have a sales action.

### §99 — Laws of design · 2003-01-26

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/99/): Rejects universal aesthetic laws, including fixed font or color counts, and discusses context-dependent exceptions.

**Application:** Treat visual recommendations as choices to justify in context. Avoid imposing a restrained palette, a fixed number of typefaces, or blanket prohibitions on cards and ornament. This does not remove functional, accessibility, factual, or user-specified constraints.

### §175 — The virtue of limitations · 2012-11-18

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/175/): A concrete brief and constraints make creative work possible; an unlimited request for the best design supplies no target.

**Application:** Use the actual brand, platform, assets, language, and edit boundary as design inputs. Do not invent restrictions or assume a rewrite is necessary.

### §178 — The rule for making rules · 2014-07-12

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/178/): Advises making a successful composition and deriving its principles, rather than forcing content into a grid established in advance.

**Application:** Work with representative content before finalizing reusable rules. In a limited edit, the existing system is part of the brief rather than something to discard.

## Concept and references

### §103 — Wanna have it like this · 2004-05-02

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/103/): A reference's purpose and reasoning matter; copying a successful company's visual trappings does not reproduce its underlying success.

**Application:** Explain what a reference solves, then adapt that reasoning. Do not promise business results from resemblance alone. Its incidental brand-history example is not used as evidence here.

### §157 — On stylized design · 2009-01-07

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/157/): A categorical, one-sentence rejection of stylized design, without an explanatory method.

**Application:** Use the user's requested influence thoughtfully, without claiming this aphorism defines a complete doctrine. It is not a reason to refuse an explicitly requested visual style or fidelity task.

### §148 — Unit of sense · 2008-03-24

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/148/): Distinguishes substantive ideas and original solutions from output volume, hours, and repeated applications of an existing design.

**Application:** Assess the contribution of a concept or structural improvement. Do not infer a rule of exactly one visual idea per page, or adopt the essay's employee-productivity quotas.

### §190 — Полочка в мозгу ("A shelf in the brain," translated title) · 2018-09-09

[Russian source](https://www.artlebedev.ru/kovodstvo/sections/190/): Uses a mental filing-cabinet metaphor to argue that original design creates unfamiliar associations, while neutral and familiar work tends to attract less discussion. The author interprets resistance to a new logo positively.

**Application:** Explore original concepts, narratives, and associations when they serve the project; do not reduce the work to matching familiar visual conventions. Evaluate novelty against the visitor's actual task. Treat the neuroscience language as the author's explanatory metaphor, not verified cognitive science, and do not equate complaints or confusion with successful design.

### §191 — Искусство ("Art," translated title) · 2019-01-10

[Russian source](https://www.artlebedev.ru/kovodstvo/sections/191/): Distinguishes experiences enjoyed in the moment from works repeatedly used for aesthetic pleasure, and states a categorical personal position about art created for museums.

**Application:** Consider both the immediate experience and the continuing value of original copy, imagery, interaction, and composition. Aesthetic enjoyment can be a legitimate project outcome. This is a philosophical position, not a website specification or a reason to impose the author's views on a cultural institution's brief.

## Information and navigation

### §56 — Information vs. presentation · 2000-07-11

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/56/): Prioritizes delivery of information, contrasts repeat-use sites with elaborate presentations, and argues for fast access to useful content even in a visual presentation.

**Application:** Match density and expression to the task. Keep routine work direct, while allowing a rich discovery experience. Historical browser limits, numeric widths, and predictions are not current technical specifications.

### §78 — Pictograms and ideograms · 2001-12-26

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/78/): Explains how symbols become ambiguous and recommends text where interpretation is difficult. Navigation should communicate location, choices, and destinations.

**Application:** Add meaningful visible labels to unfamiliar icons and distinguish controls with different functions. Do not adopt a fixed seven-item navigation limit from the essay's illustrative discussion.

### §76 — Two major principles of interface building · 2001-12-21

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/76/): Uses a stereo-control design to argue for consistent controls and synchronized feedback, extending the reasoning to software and familiar system controls.

**Application:** Give similar actions consistent behavior and update visible state when something changes. Evaluate novel presentation by whether visitors can understand and operate it, not by novelty alone.

### §83 — Secrets of good hypertext · 2002-02-18

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/83/): Discusses self-contained prose, useful destinations, meaningful anchor text, reading flow, and cross-references. It also favors collecting links separately in some reading contexts while allowing reference-work exceptions.

**Application:** Write sentences that make sense without clicking, name destinations, and link useful related material. Do not mechanically relocate every inline citation or navigation link to the footer. Avoid unnecessary repeated emphasis around links.

### §129 — News on non-news sites · 2006-01-30

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/129/): Questions habitual corporate news feeds, recommends maintaining currency, and argues against unnecessary publication dates in headings.

**Application:** Add a feed for a real editorial purpose and maintenance capacity. Preserve dates needed for currency, events, availability, and provenance. Archiving or deleting existing material still requires the relevant task scope.

## Editorial and typographic craft

### §125 — Facts · 2005-12-15

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/125/): Criticizes careless sourcing, changed quotations, inaccurate visual details, and failure to listen to clients.

**Application:** Check important claims, names, numbers, and product details against available sources. Preserve supplied meaning when editing. Flag uncertain facts instead of filling the design with invented proof.

### §136 — The proximity theory · 2006-08-24

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/136/): Spatial closeness communicates association; headings should belong visually to the following content. Position, size, weight, and color also distinguish meaning.

**Application:** Make groups clear through relationships before adding containers. Check labels, captions, prices, and actions with real content, not just evenly spaced placeholder blocks.

### §142 — Tracking for lowercase type and capitals · 2007-10-08

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/142/): Explicitly advocates increased tracking for capitals and rejects it for lowercase.

**Application:** Use this as an authored typographic position, and inspect optical spacing in the actual typeface and script. Do not transplant Latin or Cyrillic settings mechanically into Hebrew or Arabic.

### §120 — Hanging punctuation · 2005-10-19

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/120/): Describes optical alignment by letting lighter punctuation extend beyond text margins, with different treatment for different marks.

**Application:** Examine quotes, list markers, and display text for optical balance. Use robust techniques suited to the layout and writing direction; avoid per-line offsets that break responsive text. The article's historical priority claim is not independently established here.

### §135 — Informal typography · 2006-06-04

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/135/): Discusses strikethroughs and text emoticons as expressive devices, while observing that they do not replace wit or clear communication.

**Application:** Typography may carry humor, contrast, and voice when appropriate. Keep actual prices and states unambiguous; do not manufacture fake discounts or turn every heading into a joke.

### §141 — Make it short · 2007-08-05

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/141/): Revises a roadside message by removing unnecessary wording and improving reading conditions, emphasizing the context in which it is received.

**Application:** Write for the moment and audience. Concise controls and substantial technical explanations can both be correct; brevity is not a reason to delete essential information.

## Simplicity and variety

### §108 — On simplicity of design · 2004-10-14

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/108/): A short directive to make design simple, without an expanded method.

**Application:** Remove unnecessary difficulty. This alone supplies no required minimalist palette, layout, or level of detail. Read it alongside §99 and §147 rather than as an isolated universal rule.

### §147 — Simplicity ≠ primitivity · 2008-03-05

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/147/): Uses architectural detail to distinguish welcoming simplicity from impoverished design.

**Application:** Preserve character, nuance, ornament, and useful richness when they serve the experience. Simplicity need not mean reducing every page to bare text and boxes.

### §185 — Diversity · 2016-10-14

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/185/): Uses an anecdote about varying the presentation of the same material to advocate variety in design and daily life.

**Application:** Seek fresh compositions with the assets available. Avoid monotonous layouts where varied material deserves different treatment; retain repetition where comparison and predictability help. The anecdote is not used as evidence about animal care.

## Mobile chronology

### §177 — Mobile websites · 2013-09-13

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/177/): Opposes separate mobile sites and argues that smartphones can display ordinary desktop sites.

### §182 — Mobile versions of websites two years later · 2015-12-01

[Source](https://www.artlebedev.com/mandership/182/): Revises the earlier categorical rejection in light of mobile use while retaining reservations about small-screen interfaces.

**Application of the pair:** Represent the change accurately. Neither essay verifies a current implementation. Keep coherent content and functionality across devices while implementing responsive reflow, touch behavior, readable typography, and actual mobile checks. Do not attribute that whole modern practice to either article.
