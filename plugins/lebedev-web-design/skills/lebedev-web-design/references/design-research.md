# Studio evidence and reference selection

2026 projects and Russian-source coverage reviewed 2026-11-08; the earlier project and process selection retains its 2026-11-01 review. This is a selected body of evidence for website decisions, not a complete history or official studio manual. Project descriptions establish stated intent and credited work, not independent proof of commercial effectiveness. Visual and interaction observations below identify the surface inspected; none establishes the current behavior of an untested client site.

## Freshness check

For substantial new or reimagined projects, or a request for the latest studio direction:

1. Check the official [Russian web portfolio](https://www.artlebedev.ru/web/) and [news archive](https://www.artlebedev.ru/news/) for recent relevant work. The [2026 archive](https://www.artlebedev.ru/news/2026/) was used for this update; follow the archive's current-year link on future reviews. Use news and direct project links when the portfolio's dynamic interface is not available to text retrieval.
2. Consult Russian case studies alongside their available English versions. Follow the site's language links; translations and indexes need not contain identical projects or essays. Verify project publication dates and credits on the project pages. Footer years, crawl dates, and a research note's review date are not project release dates.
3. Select evidence by the problem it solves: content, architecture, selection, commercial explanation, interaction, or visual expression. Compare useful recent work with enduring examples where appropriate. Recency alone does not establish quality, relevance, or effectiveness; no fixed number of references is required.
4. Visually inspect the references that will materially shape the direction and record the evidence level below. Check the actual client experience only when its behavior is relevant and accessible. Treat a client site's later changes separately from the studio's published design.
5. Note the review date, source URLs, project dates, relevant lessons, and access or verification gaps in the project's research or handoff. Reuse this research for follow-up edits unless the brief or evidence changes. Stop once the relevant decisions are supported; do not exhaustively crawl the portfolio.

If browsing or rendering is unavailable, use the dated evidence available, explain the limitation, and avoid claiming to have checked the latest work. Refresh this skill's persistent reference library when maintaining the skill, rather than silently rewriting it during ordinary client work.

## Visual and interaction evidence

Keep four things distinct: **published description**, **visual observation**, **tested interaction**, and **application to this brief**. A visual claim needs an inspected image or rendered page; an interaction claim needs an observed action and result. A studio account of improved usability is not an independently measured outcome.

For references central to the direction, inspect the composition, typography, imagery, content density, navigation, and relevant detail or action states beyond the hero. Look at supplied mobile examples when relevant. A mobile mockup verifies that pictured composition only; testing responsive behavior requires operating the actual page at the relevant widths. Likewise, a case-study animation or embedded demo does not establish production behavior.

Record a compact observation: URL and date; client artifact, case-study wrapper, embedded demo, or live site; view or state and viewport/capture size where known; observed detail; action and result if tested; remaining limits. A screenshot or precise state description can support the record. Keep research captures separate from production assets and do not redistribute proprietary client imagery as the new site's media.

## Project accounts

### Kerranova — material selection as an experience

[Russian case study](https://www.artlebedev.ru/kerranova/), 2026-10-27. Art director: Pavel Gerasimchuk. Designer: Alena Sofyina.

**Source:** Material samples and formats lead into the catalog; product options update without reloading; room views keep products and indicative costs visible. The account describes restrained animation, dimensional tile views, and sound.

**Visual observation — 2026-11-08:** In the embedded client homepage, a warm light surface, large spaced wordmark, handwritten display line, material photography, and compact text navigation establish the composition. A separate tiled material demonstration makes surface variation visible.

**Tested interaction:** In the studio case study, activating "Перемешать плитки" (shuffle tiles) changed the displayed tile-image arrangement. This verifies that embedded demonstration only. Room rotation, sound, production configuration, and mobile behavior were not tested. Browser capture: 1265 × 712.

**Application:** Design the content, assets, and selection tools around how visitors judge materials. Original product demonstrations can carry both the concept and a practical decision; the site's quiet palette is not a universal prescription.

### Arnezi — identity organizing an assortment

[Russian case study](https://www.artlebedev.ru/arnezi/site/), 2026-09-14. Art director: Vova Zlidnev. The case study labels the site as being populated by the client.

**Source:** Brand triangles and category colors support navigation; filters and product badges aid selection; product pages include specifications, purchase locations, and photographs. The account describes use on phones and large office screens.

**Visual observation — 2026-11-08:** Embedded examples show orange, green, and blue triangular product fields above black category panels with text subcategories. Narrow mobile compositions carry the same geometry, category lists, and contrasting new-product sections. Browser capture: 1265 × 712.

**Verification limit:** These are client-design examples inside the portfolio. Filters, links, live content completeness, and responsive reflow were not tested.

**Application:** Identity can organize a substantial catalog rather than merely decorate its header. Build category names, product information, navigation, and original promotional media as one system. Retain text labels so color is not the only way to distinguish categories.

### START — the complete subscription experience

[Russian case study](https://www.artlebedev.ru/start/site/), 2026-06-15. Art director: Vova Zlidnev. Designers: Sofya Cherepakhina, Nikita Kolyugin, and Yana Novikova.

**Source:** The project revisits the promotional landing, subscription terms, personalized discovery, catalog filters, viewing, editorial content, profiles, and account management. The studio also designed the mobile version and a separate smart-TV application.

**Visual observation — 2026-11-08:** The embedded landing combines dark surfaces, red accents, large display headings, and film imagery. A subscription screen aligns dated periods and their charges on a horizontal timeline directly above payment choices. Browser capture: 1265 × 712.

**Verification limit:** Pricing and controls were inspected as case-study imagery; checkout, personalization, account behavior, and mobile operation were not tested. Pictured prices are historical design content, not current offers.

**Application:** Treat acquisition, understandable commercial terms, discovery, and continued use as one website problem. Clear explanation of payment timing can be part of conversion work; neither the visuals nor the studio account proves a conversion uplift.

### MPSTATS — explaining, selecting, and using software

[Russian case study](https://www.artlebedev.ru/mpstats/), 2026-03-31. Art director: Evgeny Panov. Designer: Bogdan Yukin.

**Source:** The site explains a marketplace software platform, lets visitors choose modules or bundles, distinguishes them in the cart, and presents summaries and educational material in the account area.

**Visual observation — 2026-11-08:** A pale-blue cloud groups magenta dimensional icons. The embedded selection screen uses a large explanatory headline, marketplace and task filters, black active pills, and colorful module or bundle cards on a light surface. Browser capture: 1265 × 712.

**Verification limit:** The observed selection screen is a case-study presentation. Filtering, purchasing, account calculations, and live responsiveness were not tested.

**Application:** Originate a coherent product explanation, asset language, selection architecture, and account journey. A modular grid can support varied purchase scenarios without making every section identical. Icons and marketing illustrations still need clear labels and grounded product claims.

### Earlier project selection

The following entries retain the observations recorded on 2026-11-01; they were not newly visually verified in the 2026-11-08 update.

### Tasty Coffee — editorial scale and expertise

[Wholesale website](https://www.artlebedev.com/tastycoffee/site/), 2024-04-19. Art director: Sergey Kulinkovich.

**Source:** Large photography, video, expressive typography, production transparency, detailed coffee information, educational interactions, maps, and roasting reports.

**Visual observation:** The embedded client hero combines a production scene with large condensed Cyrillic lettering and contrasting weights.

**Application:** A subject with depth can combine an expressive introduction with useful expert content. The type choice and scale are specific to this project, not a template for a catalog or account screen.

### Gran Re — material and origin

[Cheese factory website](https://www.artlebedev.com/gran-re/site/), 2024-06-03. Art director: Erken Kagarov.

**Source:** Cheese imagined as the Ural Mountains, bespoke typography, and curves recalling cheese forms; the client assembled and populated the website.

**Visual observation:** Cheese cutouts overlap warm yellow rounded fields against a near-black background.

**Application:** Material and origin can become a coherent visual premise. The observation supports this particular composition, not a mandatory palette or permission to reuse its assets.

### Raketa — technical detail as experience

[Watch factory website](https://www.artlebedev.com/raketa/site/), 2025-08-27. Art director: Sergey Kulinkovich.

**Source:** Explorable mechanisms with rotation and sound, boutique-inspired environments, and a modular technical grid that also accommodates mobile content.

**Visual observation:** An organized array of isolated watch components on a white field. The interactive behavior was not tested in this research.

**Application:** Mechanics and detail can organize a rich experience. Choose the representation for the brief; neither a 3D explorer nor a static substitute is automatically preferable.

### Diagnocat — demonstrate and continue

[Promo website](https://www.artlebedev.com/diagnocat/site/), 2025-07-17. Art director: Evgeny Panov.

**Source:** An analysis demonstration, comparison blocks, an available demo action, audience-specific explanations, and graphics extending the existing identity. Text reviewed; no visual verification recorded.

**Application:** Make the service understandable through a demonstration and an appropriate next step. Preserve established product identity when that is the brief; do not transfer its medical claims to another product.

### Metallservice — commerce with a large assortment

[Online store](https://www.artlebedev.com/metallservice/site/), 2024-09-16. Art director: Pavel Gerasimchuk.

**Source:** Banners explain retail quantities; a category tree organizes products; promotions appear within catalog results; product configurators support order parameters.

**Visual observation:** The case-study catalog view has a prominent search field and a multi-column category hierarchy, with blue branding on a light surface.

**Application:** Findability, units, and configuration can be the principal design contribution. Product cards and restrained banners are legitimate tools when they serve that structure.

### MIPT — an information-rich institution

[University website](https://www.artlebedev.com/mipt/), 2024-02-22. Art director: Artemy Lebedev.

**Source:** Audience tabs change homepage content; departments contribute to a news system; custom characters and division-based patterns connect sections and schools.

**Visual observation:** An embedded editorial composition places strongly differentiated text headlines beside a large campus photograph. Audience-tab functionality was not tested.

**Application:** A site can have several equally legitimate audiences and destinations. Identity, news, and substantial information need not become a single marketing funnel. A maintained news feed is a purposeful feature here.

## Process evidence

### Delcredere — find the relevant idea through iteration

[Making of the website](https://www.artlebedev.com/delcredere/site/process/).

**Source:** Begins with discussion, user scenarios, a site map, and content planning. The team re-examines client knowledge, explores different directions, recognizes the visitor's need for reassurance, and develops a sea-to-teacup metaphor through prototypes and crafted assets.

**Application:** Discovery and iteration should be proportional to the assignment. A visitor's emotional concern or a brand association can generate the concept; the subject need not be a literal physical product. This account does not impose its internal tools, meetings, or approval sequence on every task.

### Bank of Russia — respect a limited brief

[Making of version 3.5](https://www.artlebedev.com/cbr/site3.5/process/).

**Source:** The assignment is to adopt a new identity without structural changes. The team studies guidelines, adjusts mobile layouts and icons, and adds document dates at the client's request.

**Application:** A focused restyle can be complete without a new hero, metaphor, or information architecture. Its separate accessibility version is historical implementation evidence, not a requirement to segregate accessibility today.

### Demilie — develop and optimize expressive motion

[Making of version 2.0](https://www.artlebedev.com/demilie/site2/process/).

**Source:** Concept exploration progresses through animation and browser prototypes, collaboration with technical specialists, 3D implementation, mobile work, and file-size and motion optimization.

**Application:** Motion and dimensional imagery may be essential art direction rather than expendable decoration. Test feasibility and delivery as part of the concept. Do not copy historical libraries or asset pipelines without assessing the current project.

## Evidence boundaries

The essays, studio statements, and individual cases inform recommendations; they do not establish one required palette, font count, layout density, or number of ideas. Preserve source distinctions in explanations. Research notes should not imply studio endorsement, live-site verification, or measured conversion gains.
